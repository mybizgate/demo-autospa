What to expect at every visit

We listen first.

Before touching the car we ask what bothers you about it and what result you are hoping for, then recommend only the work that gets you there.

We explain the work.

You will know which products go on the car, how long each stage takes and what the finish will realistically look like.

Helpful answers

Before you enquire

No hard sell and no instant “confirmed” booking. Share the car and the result you want, then the studio can advise the next step.

How long will the service take?

Timing depends on the vehicle condition and selected work. Ask on WhatsApp and confirm the expected handover time before your appointment.

What affects the final price?

Vehicle size, paint or interior condition, preparation required and the chosen protection all affect the quote. The prices shown here are illustrative starting points.

Does sending the form confirm my appointment?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Your appointment is only confirmed after the studio replies and agrees on the date, time and work.

What should I send for a faster quote?

Include the vehicle make and model, the area you want improved and clear photos where useful. The studio may still recommend an in-person inspection.
