Services

Ceramic coating and paint protection

Multi-stage decontamination and preparation, then a ceramic coating that holds gloss, resists water spotting and makes routine washing easier.

Paint correction

Machine polishing to reduce swirl marks, light scratches and haze, returning clarity and depth to the paint before any protection goes on.

Interior refinement

A deep clean of seats, carpets, trim and the touchpoints you use every day, with leather cleaned and conditioned rather than just wiped.

Maintenance detail

A careful hand wash, light decontamination and a tidy interior, tyre and trim finish. The right choice for a car that is already well looked after.

What to expect at every visit

We listen first.

Before touching the car we ask what bothers you about it and what result you are hoping for, then recommend only the work that gets you there.

We explain the work.

You will know which products go on the car, how long each stage takes and what the finish will realistically look like.

We finish properly.

Door shuts, badges, wheel barrels and glass edges get the same attention as the bonnet. The car is inspected under lights before handover.
